A-03

Details

Enlarged · Tokopedia

Buyer-facing commerce surfaces. Delivered and maintained the server-driven homepage and discovery pages, whose layout updates ship without an app release. Built the gamification mission floater, a new-user Lottie widget, product bundling pages, and product recommendation surfaces. Adopted Lynx to serve a single widget implementation across Android and iOS.

Built and maintained product management flows — creation, editing, drafts, duplication — and delivered promotional tooling for campaign, coupon, and flash sale management. Contributed to the large-screen initiative supporting tablets and foldables on the add/edit product module.

Maintained Tokopedia's Android design system library, used across every feature. Integrated it with ByteDance's design system post-acquisition and migrated distribution from JFrog to ByteDance's internal CDN. Led APK size reduction — removing unused modules, converting to dynamic delivery — stabilised homepage rendering with asset and Lottie caching, introduced real-time bottom navigation updates, and co-led the dark mode working group for all owned features.
