A Few Accomplishments

Tokopedia — Homepage & Discovery

Buyer-facing commerce surfaces on Tokopedia (PT. Tokopedia, Dec 2019 – Jul 2026). Built the gamification mission floater, a new-user Lottie widget, product bundling pages, and product recommendation surfaces. Tokopedia — Seller Platform

Merchant-facing tooling on Tokopedia. Built and maintained product management flows (creation, editing, drafts, duplication) and delivered promotional tooling for campaign, coupon, and flash sale management. Tokopedia — Android Design System & Tech Initiatives

Maintained Tokopedia's Android design system library, used across all features. Integrated it with ByteDance's design system post-acquisition and migrated the library distribution from JFrog to ByteDance's internal CDN. Led APK size reduction (removing unused modules, converting to dynamic delivery), stabilised home page rendering performance with asset and Lottie caching, introduced real-time bottom navigation updates, and co-led the dark mode working group for all owned features.
